1. # **Gamified Rewards & Recognition System Policy**

# **1.1 Purpose of the System**

* Linknbit is introducing a Gamified Rewards & Recognition System to improve employee motivation, productivity, and workplace engagement.  
* This system turns daily office work into a structured “quest-based” experience where employees complete tasks, earn points, get recognized for performance, and redeem rewards.

The goal is simple:

Complete tasks \= Points \= Rewards \+ Recognition

# **1.2 System Overview**

* Employees log in to a digital gamified portal.  
* HR and managers post tasks (quests).  
* Employees claim and complete tasks.  
* Points are earned after approval.  
* Points are stored in a monthly wallet that resets at the end of each month.  
* Employees can redeem points for rewards.

# **1.3 Currency System (Link Points \- LP)**

| Item | Value |
| :---- | :---- |
| 1 Link Point (LP) | PKR 10 |
| Monthly LP reset | Yes (starts fresh every month) |

* Monthly earning target is approximately 500 Link Points (LP) per employee. This is a soft benchmark used for system balance and budget planning, not a strict limit.   
* High-performing employees may exceed this based on high task activity and approved shoutouts; however, overall point distribution remains naturally controlled through task availability, approval flow, and HR-managed shoutouts. 

# **1.4 How Employees Earn Points**

Employees can earn Link Points through 2 main sources:

## 1.4.1 Task Completion (Main Source)

Employees claim tasks from the Quest Board, complete them, and submit proof.

| Task Type | Example | Points |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Easy Task | Meme idea/creation, small office help | 20–30 LP |
| Medium Task | Lunch order, event decoration help | 40–60 LP |
| Hard Task | Project support, urgent help | 70–100 LP |

Employees can complete multiple tasks at the same time.

## 1.4.2 Shoutouts (Performance Recognition)

Shoutouts are controlled, performance-based rewards issued by: 

* Manager  
* Team Lead  
* HR

Each shoutout must include a clear written justification (1–2 lines minimum) explaining the specific contribution or impact. HR will review the justification before approval to ensure fairness and consistency.

| Shoutout Type | Points |
| :---- | :---- |
| Standard Performance Shoutout | 100 LP |
| High Impact Contribution | 120–150 LP |

Examples:

* Solving critical client issues.  
* Handling emergency situations.  
* Exceptional cross-team support.

## 1.4.3 Eligibility for High Rewards (Behavior & Performance Rule)

To qualify for high-value rewards (including cash rewards and premium redemptions), employees must maintain good overall performance and behavior during the month, not just earn points.

This includes:

* Regular attendance and punctuality.

* Positive and professional workplace behavior.

* Following company policies and rules.

* No major disciplinary or performance issues.

* Consistent cooperation and responsibility in assigned work.

- This will be evaluated by HR and managers based on day-to-day observation, attendance records, and overall work behavior during the month.   
- Only employees who meet these behavioral and performance standards will be approved for high-value reward redemptions, even if they have enough Link Points.

# **1.5 Important Rule: Monthly Reset System**

* All LP reset to zero every month.  
* Employees start fresh each month.  
* Encourages consistent performance instead of accumulation.

# **1.6 Reputation Points (Long-Term Recognition)**

In addition to LP, employees also earn Reputation Points:

* These are NOT redeemable.  
* They do NOT reset monthly.  
* They are visible on employee profiles.  
* Link Points (LP) are used for monthly rewards, while Reputation Points reflect long-term performance, consistency, and overall contribution.   
* Reputation Points are collected from all approved tasks/activities and shoutouts as a long-term record of performance, separate from monthly Link Points. 

Purpose:

Reputation Points are maintained to evaluate an employee’s long-term value and performance within the organization. They are not based on short-term tasks or monthly activity, but on consistent contribution over time. 

Reputation Points are used for the following purposes (but not limited to):

* To monitor long-term performance consistency of employees over months and years.  
* To assess overall employee value and contribution level within the organization.  
* To support retention decisions, helping identify high-value employees for long-term growth and stability.  
* To assist in salary increment decisions based on sustained performance and contribution trends.  
* To evaluate increment requests, where HR can review Reputation Points to understand the employee’s performance history and impact.

# **1.8 Cash Reward Policy**

* Cash rewards can only be redeemed at a minimum of 500 LP.  
* Employees must request redemption in the system.  
* Request requires HR approval.  
* HR verifies performance \+ eligibility before payout. 

Important:

* Cash rewards are limited to employees who achieve at least 500 LP in a given month. If one or more employees reach this threshold, they become eligible for cash reward consideration. Among eligible employees, rewards will be distributed based on ranking of highest total Link Points earned in that month.  
* Top performers are defined as employees with the highest total Link Points (LP) earned in a given month relative to others.   
* Final approval remains subject to HR verification of performance, behavior, and eligibility criteria.

# **1.11 System Discipline & Participation Restrictions**

To maintain accountability and quality of work:

* In cases of serious mistakes (e.g., critical project errors, negligence, or failure in important assigned responsibilities), employees may be temporarily restricted from participating in the gamified system.  
* This restriction may include:  
  * Temporary restriction from participating in the gamified system, including claiming tasks and earning Link Points (LP).   
* The duration of restriction will depend on the severity of the issue.  
* Severity will be determined based on the level of impact on work, team, or business operations.   
* All decisions will be reviewed and finalized by HR and management.

Note: This is not a penalty on past earned points, but a temporary restriction on participation to ensure responsibility and fairness.

# **1.12 System Rules Summary**

* No negative LP deduction in the normal system.  
* Tasks must be claimed before doing them.  
* Tasks completed without prior claiming may not be eligible for Link Points unless approved by HR under special circumstances.   
* Proof (such as photo, screenshot, or manager confirmation) may be required depending on the nature of the task.   
* HR/Manager approval required for validation.  
* Multiple tasks can be active at the same time.  
* Tasks must be completed within the defined timeframe. Incomplete or delayed tasks may expire and will not be eligible for points.   
* Task completion timelines will be defined individually for each task at the time of posting. Deadlines may vary based on urgency and nature of the task.   
* Monthly reset applies to LP only (not reputation).

# **1.13 Simple Understanding of System**

* Employees complete tasks, earn points, receive rare performance shoutouts, and redeem rewards.  
* Points reset monthly, but reputation points reflect long-term performance.

# **1.14 Final Note**

This system is designed to:

* Encourage productivity.  
* Recognize real performance.  
* Create fair competition.  
* Provide recognition without increasing fixed salary costs.   
* Build a motivated workplace culture.

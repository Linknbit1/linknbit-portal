## Method

- The week runs Monday 31 August 00:00 to Friday 4 September, office timezone (`Asia/Karachi`).
- A task appears in the main table if **at least one timer session overlapped the window**, whatever
  its status. Sessions are clipped to the window, so one crossing midnight is split across the right
  days and never double-counted.
- Only tasks on a **development** service block are counted. Design, marketing and SEO ran timers too
  and are out of scope for this document.
- Per-task and per-person figures are rounded to the minute independently, so the person column sums
  six minutes higher than the task column.
- Status is the task's state now, not its state when the timer ran.
- Release notes come from the portal's changelog and repository history, not from task records.

### The two clocks — read this before comparing hours

The portal measures the week two independent ways and they do not agree:

- **Timer hours** — a stopwatch run against a specific task. Precise, but only some of the team
  use it. Marketing and SEO logged **zero** timer time this week; that means the timer was not
  running, not that no work happened.
- **Standup hours** — what each person wrote up at the end of the day. Broad coverage, but
  self-reported and rounded.

They are never added together. Where a number below is one or the other, it says so.

## Method

- **Week** is Monday 17 Aug 00:00 to the end of Friday 21 Aug, in the office timezone
  (`Asia/Karachi`). Saturday 22nd is not included.
- **"Worked on"** means the task record changed during the window — status, assignment, content or
  dates. It is the broadest honest definition available; the portal keeps no per-field task history
  to narrow it further.
- **"Completed / approved"** counts tasks in `completed` or `approved` state that changed during the
  window. A task approved this week but finished last week will appear here.
- **Timer hours** are clipped to the window, so a session that crossed midnight is split across the
  right days and never double-counted.
- **Standup hours** are self-reported totals from filed standups, counted at project and person
  level only. Just under half of this week's standup entries name a specific task; the rest name
  only a project or nothing at all. Splitting 165h by service off that minority would produce
  figures that look precise and are not, so no per-service standup number is given anywhere.
- **🆕** marks a task created during this week.
- Soft-deleted tasks and projects are excluded throughout.
